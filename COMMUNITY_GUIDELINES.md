## 🎯 Mission Statement

Rumour is a hyper-local discovery platform for real, spontaneous events. Our community thrives on authentic, safe, and respectful interactions. These guidelines help maintain a welcoming environment for everyone.

---

## ✅ What We Love (Encouraged Content)

### Local Events & Gatherings
- ✅ Art exhibitions, gallery openings, pop-up shows
- ✅ Music performances, DJ sets, live bands
- ✅ Food events, pop-up restaurants, tasting sessions
- ✅ Gaming tournaments, LAN parties, board game nights
- ✅ Sports meetups, pickup games, fitness classes
- ✅ Community gatherings, networking events, social mixers
- ✅ Educational workshops, talks, skill-sharing sessions

### Event Details We Encourage
- 📍 Accurate location information
- ⏰ Clear timing and duration
- 🎫 Transparent entry requirements (free, ticketed, password-protected)
- 👥 Appropriate age restrictions when necessary
- ♿ Accessibility information
- 🎨 Authentic photos of the venue or past events

## ❌ Prohibited Content (Auto-Block)

### 1. Hate Speech & Discrimination
**Zero Tolerance** - Immediate removal and account suspension

- ❌ Racist, sexist, homophobic, transphobic, or ableist content
- ❌ Religious intolerance or attacks
- ❌ Ethnic slurs or derogatory language
- ❌ Symbols or imagery associated with hate groups
- ❌ Content targeting protected characteristics (race, gender, religion, disability, nationality, sexual orientation)

**Examples**:
- "No [ethnicity] allowed"
- Use of racial or ethnic slurs
- Gender-based exclusions or mockery
- Religious stereotyping or attacks

---

### 2. Harassment & Threats
**Zero Tolerance** - Immediate removal and account suspension

- ❌ Threats of violence or harm
- ❌ Doxxing (sharing private information)
- ❌ Targeted harassment of individuals
- ❌ Bullying or intimidation
- ❌ Encouraging self-harm or suicide
- ❌ Stalking or unwanted contact

**Examples**:
- "I'm going to find and hurt you"
- Sharing someone's home address or phone number
- Repeated targeting of a specific person
- Encouraging dangerous behavior

---

### 3. Illegal Activities
**Zero Tolerance** - Immediate removal, account suspension, and potential law enforcement reporting

- ❌ Drug sales or distribution (excluding legally prescribed medications)
- ❌ Weapons sales (firearms, explosives, dangerous weapons)
- ❌ Human trafficking or exploitation
- ❌ Underage drinking or substance use events
- ❌ Prostitution or sexual services
- ❌ Stolen goods, counterfeit items
- ❌ Illegal gambling operations
- ❌ Fraud, scams, or pyramid schemes

**Examples**:
- "Selling [illegal substance]"
- "Need a fake ID? Meet here"
- "Unauthorized rave at government property"
- "Betting pool for cash prizes" (unlicensed)

---

### 4. Sexual Content & Exploitation
**Zero Tolerance** - Immediate removal and account suspension

- ❌ Sexual or nude imagery
- ❌ Sexual services or solicitation
- ❌ Content sexualizing minors
- ❌ Pornographic material
- ❌ Explicit sexual descriptions
- ❌ Unwanted sexual advances or content

**Examples**:
- "18+ strip show at [location]"
- "Looking for hookups at this event"
- Sexually explicit event descriptions
- Inappropriate images or emojis suggesting sexual content

---

### 5. Violence & Dangerous Activities
**Zero Tolerance** - Immediate removal

- ❌ Graphic violence or gore
- ❌ Glorification of violence
- ❌ Instructions for making weapons or explosives
- ❌ Organized fighting events (unlicensed)
- ❌ Animal abuse or cruelty
- ❌ Dangerous stunts encouraging harm

**Examples**:
- "Street fight at midnight"
- "Rooftop jumping challenge"
- Detailed plans for vandalism
- Promoting reckless driving events

---

### 6. Personal Information (PII)
**Auto-Block** - Privacy protection

- ❌ Full names (first + last name)
- ❌ Home addresses
- ❌ Phone numbers
- ❌ Email addresses in event descriptions
- ❌ Social security numbers, ID numbers
- ❌ Credit card or financial information
- ❌ Medical records or private health information

**Examples**:
- "Contact John Smith at 555-1234"
- "My apartment is at 123 Main St, Apt 4B"
- "Email me at john@example.com"

**Note**: Venue addresses for public events are allowed. Business contact info posted by verified businesses is allowed.

---

### 7. Spam & Commercial Exploitation
**Auto-Block** - Excessive commercial activity

- ❌ Multi-level marketing (MLM) recruitment
- ❌ Cryptocurrency schemes or pump-and-dumps
- ❌ Repeated identical posts
- ❌ External link farms or click-bait
- ❌ Fake engagement schemes ("Like for prize")
- ❌ Affiliate marketing spam
- ❌ Unauthorized commercial advertising

**Examples**:
- "Join my team and earn $$$"
- "Buy followers, guaranteed results"
- "Click here for free iPhone"
- Posting the same event 10 times

**Note**: Legitimate business events (pop-up shops, food trucks, ticketed shows) are allowed if they're actual events at specific locations.

---

### 8. Misinformation & Deception
**Auto-Remove** - Protecting community trust

- ❌ Impersonation of others
- ❌ Fake emergency alerts
- ❌ False venue information
- ❌ Misleading event details
- ❌ Location spoofing (posting far from actual location)
- ❌ Fake verification claims

**Examples**:
- "Government shutdown at City Hall" (false)
- "Free concert by [famous artist]" (when not true)
- Creating event at one location while posting from another
- Pretending to be a venue owner or celebrity

## ⚠️ Flagged for Review (Manual Moderation)

These require human judgment:

### Potentially Problematic Content
- ⚠️ Excessive profanity (context-dependent)
- ⚠️ Political events (allowed, but monitored for hate speech)
- ⚠️ Religious gatherings (allowed, but monitored for extremism)
- ⚠️ Controversial topics (free speech vs. safety balance)
- ⚠️ Alcohol-related events (legal drinking age required)
- ⚠️ Edge cases not covered by auto-moderation

## 🛡️ AI Moderation Guidelines

### For AI Moderators Reviewing Content:

**REJECT if content contains**:
1. **Hate Speech Indicators**:
   - Slurs against protected groups
   - Dehumanizing language
   - Calls for violence or exclusion
   - Symbols of hate groups

2. **Safety Threats**:
   - Direct threats ("I will hurt...")
   - Doxxing (sharing addresses, phone numbers)
   - Encouragement of illegal activity
   - Sexual exploitation or solicitation

3. **Personal Information**:
   - Full names with contact details
   - Home addresses
   - Phone numbers or email addresses
   - Financial information

4. **Spam Patterns**:
   - MLM recruitment language
   - "Get rich quick" schemes
   - Excessive external links
   - Repeated identical content

**APPROVE if content**:
- Describes a legitimate local event
- Provides location and time details
- Uses appropriate language
- Doesn't violate any of the above categories
- Venue/business contact info (if appropriate)

**FLAG FOR MANUAL REVIEW if**:
- Context is unclear
- Borderline profanity
- Political/religious content (allowed but monitored)
- New slang that might be coded hate speech
- Uncertainty about intent

## 📊 Enforcement Actions

### Severity Levels

**Level 1: Auto-Block (No Post Created)**
- Hate speech
- Illegal content
- Sexual exploitation
- Severe threats
- PII exposure

**Level 2: Pending Manual Review**
- Ambiguous content
- Borderline cases
- First-time profanity
- Technical moderation failures

**Level 3: Community Flags (3 flags = auto-remove)**
- User-reported violations
- Community standards enforcement
- Proximity-gated flagging (must be within 100m)

**Level 4: Account Actions**
- First violation: Warning
- Second violation: 7-day suspension
- Third violation: 30-day suspension
- Fourth violation: Permanent ban

### When Profanity is Acceptable
- ✅ Band names (e.g., "Holy F*** playing live")
- ✅ Art exhibition titles (when artistic expression)

- ❌ Excessive or gratuitous

## 📞 Reporting Violations

Users can flag content if:
- They're within 100m of the event location
- Content violates these guidelines
- They believe it's harmful or inappropriate

**False flagging** may result in:
- Reduced flag weight in the system
- Account warnings for abuse
- Suspension for coordinated false flagging

## 💡 Best Practices for Event Creators

### Do:
- ✅ Be specific about your event
- ✅ Include accurate location and time
- ✅ Mention any costs or requirements
- ✅ Use appropriate, welcoming language
- ✅ Add photos of the venue (if available)
- ✅ Respect privacy - use usernames, not real names

### Don't:
- ❌ Spam the map with duplicate events
- ❌ Mislead about what's happening
- ❌ Post far from the actual event location
- ❌ Use clickbait titles
- ❌ Include excessive profanity
- ❌ Share personal contact information
